 Phishing Awareness Training

## Objective
Teach users to recognize phishing emails, fake websites, and social-engineering attempts.

## How to use
Open `index.html` in a browser. Read the examples and complete the interactive quiz. The module runs locally and does not collect or transmit answers.

## Topics covered
- Sender and domain verification
- Urgency, threats, and unexpected attachments
- Fake login pages and suspicious links
- Social engineering and impersonation
- Safe reporting and multi-factor authentication
- Knowledge-check quiz with feedback

## Suggested demonstration
1. Open the module in a browser.
2. Ask a colleague to identify red flags in the sample email.
3. Complete the quiz and discuss the explanations.
4. Explain how to report suspicious messages to the organization's IT/security team.
